## Project summary

Vocalis is a GitHub web app automating the Feynman Technique. Users upload documents (PDF/PPT/Word/JPEG) verified by an LLM against web sources. Students explain concepts via audio or text. A Socratic AI, acting as a curious student interrogates them with questions to fix logical gaps, ultimately scoring their knowledge accuracy and consistency.

**1. What made you choose this problem?**

When we revise alone, re-reading and highlighting feels productive, but we only find out what we do not understand when someone asks us to explain it. The Feynman technique fixes this, but it needs a patient listener, and a student studying alone does not have one. We wanted to build that listener, and make it work in the languages students actually think in.

**2. Who is it for, and how would they use it in a normal week?**

Vocalis is for a college student who revises from their own lecture notes, slides or photos of a textbook, usually alone. In a normal week they upload the notes for their next test, pick a topic and teach it to the AI by voice, text or whiteboard, and answer five follow-up questions. They read the gap report and the revision notes, in English or in Kannada, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu or Malayalam, then re-teach only the topics they missed and watch their score change on the retry. Their notes stay private to their own account, and they use their own free Gemini key.

**3. If you had another month, what would you build next?**

First, fully Kannada sessions, with the questions, the spoken voice and the reports in Kannada, then Hindi, Tamil, Telugu and Malayalam, because today only dictation and the revision notes are multilingual. Second, a teacher dashboard that shows which topics a whole class struggles with, without exposing any student's notes. Third, spaced revision reminders on each student's weak topics, with progress over time. Fourth, a lighter low-bandwidth mode for learners with limited access, and a larger pilot with a proper before-and-after comparison.
